01 • Who We Are

Committed to Financial Inclusion for the Active Poor

Victory Empowerment Centre (VEC) is a micro-finance Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) established in 2013 with a primary objective of improving the living standards of low-income women through innovative, flexible microfinance services.

Our methodology is modeled on international best practices in microfinance delivery, adapted to local Nigerian market realities. Groups are formed on a self-selection basis; while group solidarity provides mutual backing, loan repayment remains the individual's direct responsibility.

02 • Official Products

VEC Microfinance Loan Solutions

Initial disbursements start progressively and scale automatically with timely repayments.

Progressive Tier System

6 Accredited Micro-Credit Facilities

Peer Guarantee

Group Loan

Designed for 5 to 20 market women who cross-guarantee each other without traditional collateral.

  • ✓ Initial loan starts at ₦40,000
  • ✓ Increases in subsequent cycles
  • ✓ Requires registered group
Fast Turnover

Daily Loan

For traders handling daily cash turnover. Avoids stress by enabling tiny daily repayments from daily sales.

  • ✓ Initial loan starts at ₦70,000
  • ✓ Daily micro installments
  • ✓ Fast restock turnaround
Base-of-Pyramid

Bottom Loan

Low-interest loans designed specifically for petty traders at the grassroot foundation of the pyramid.

  • ✓ Low percentage interest
  • ✓ Foundation micro-credit
  • ✓ For petty trade restock
Food & Produce

Perishable Loan

Structured for traders selling vegetables, fruits, fish, and meat that need fast inventory restocking.

  • ✓ Fast approval & disbursal
  • ✓ Aligned with produce sales
  • ✓ Short, flexible cycles
Business Scaling

SME Loan

Empowers registered shops and wholesalers to purchase equipment, restock aisles, and scale operations.

  • ✓ Capital up to ₦2,000,000+
  • ✓ Monthly cash flow schedule
  • ✓ Free financial advisory
Direct Credit

Individual Loan

Tailored for proven business owners seeking direct funds without joining a cooperative lending group.

  • ✓ Higher credit thresholds
  • ✓ Direct guarantor structure
  • ✓ Dedicated officer support
